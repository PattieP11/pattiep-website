What we collect

When you send a message through the form on this site, we collect the details you type in: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and your message. We also note whether you gave permission to be called or texted.

We do not use advertising trackers on this site. The site's host may keep standard technical logs, such as your browser type and the time of your visit.

We use your information only to answer you and to help with your real estate questions. What we don't do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone else, except service providers that help deliver and store your messages, or when the law requires it.
