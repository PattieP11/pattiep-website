About Pattie

A partner, not a salesperson

Pattie Philibosian guides Petaluma homeowners through one of life's biggest transitions: leaving the family home for something smaller or single-level. She leads Pattie Philibosian Real Estate with eXp Realty, and her promise is simple: committed to your success.

Through her Committed Partner Plan, Pattie reviews each home's condition, arranges a pre-listing inspection, and designs a clear plan for repairs, updates, and timing. Then she manages the details personally, from selecting paint and fixtures to coordinating vendors, so each home goes to market as a Success-Ready Home.

She works with a small number of clients at a time, so every one gets her personal attention. Her clients hear the same reassurance again and again: "Don't worry about it. I'll take care of it. I'll help you."
